How It Works

What is Tradey?

Tradey is an interactive world map that tracks tariffs, sanctions, banned goods, and military or trade war conflicts for countries around the world.

How do I use it?

Click "Dive In" to open the map. Hover a country for a quick preview, or click it to pin the details open — you can then drag the panel anywhere and click through to sources. Countries without tracked data will simply show "No active measures tracked."

How current is the data?

Each country's entry includes a "last updated" date. Tradey is manually researched and updated periodically — it is not a live, real-time feed.

Whose perspective is this?

The tariffs, sanctions, and restrictions shown here are framed from the perspective and interests of the United States. They do not necessarily reflect how other countries view the fairness, legality, or legitimacy of these same measures.
